# Roadmap

Open directions for the German vocabulary trainer. Each item leaves this file when it ships; git keeps finished plans. Every item has an issue with its plan.

## Direction

A quick, free vocabulary trainer that runs in any browser with no account: practise both directions in three modes, bring back the words you miss, and fetch new topics online when the bundled list runs out. Stay dependency-free and keep progress in the browser.

## Now

**Umlauts without German keys** (stated 2026-11-19, [#8](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Deutsch-Studieren/issues/8)). `tuer` and `strasse` should count, as German typists write them. *Done when* typed answers using ae, oe, ue, or ss are accepted, the real spelling is shown, and a test covers it.

## Next

**How articles count for nouns** (stated 2026-11-19, [#9](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Deutsch-Studieren/issues/9)). The noun without its article is marked wrong with no hint; waits on a decision between strict, lenient, and a gender mode.

**Tests in CI** (stated 2026-11-19, [#10](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Deutsch-Studieren/issues/10)). The workflow builds Jekyll and never runs the tests.

## Later

**One product name** (stated 2026-11-19, [#11](https://github.com/UNKNOWN-X0/Deutsch-Studieren/issues/11)). The app says "German Practice", the repository "Deutsch Studieren".

## Not planned

**Accounts or cloud sync.** Progress stays in the browser.
